Who We Are

A student community driven by technology and innovation.

CUET Computer Club is a student-driven technology community at the Chittagong University of Engineering & Technology. The club provides a platform for students to explore technology, develop practical skills and connect with others who share similar interests.

Through technical events, workshops, competitions, projects and collaborative activities, we encourage students to transform their curiosity into practical knowledge and meaningful experiences.

Learn

Build practical technical knowledge.

Collaborate

Connect and work with fellow students.

Innovate

Turn ideas into meaningful solutions.

Lead

Grow into confident technology leaders.

Our Purpose

Driven by purpose, built for impact.

We aim to create an environment where students can learn, experiment, collaborate and turn their ideas into meaningful technological solutions.

Our Mission

To empower students with practical knowledge, collaborative opportunities and a supportive community that encourages continuous learning, technical excellence and responsible innovation.

Our Vision

To build a strong student technology community at CUET where curiosity becomes capability, ideas become projects and students grow into confident technology leaders.

What We Focus On

Developing skills, creating opportunities.

CUET Computer Club focuses on creating meaningful opportunities for students to learn, practice, compete and collaborate through technology.
